How to create a list, add a task and share it with your team.

Lists

Open the dashboard and give your list a name. A list keeps the work of one project together.

Tasks

Type in the New task box and press the button next to it. The task appears at the end of your list.

Teams

On the Team plan, invite people by email. They see the same lists and can be given tasks.
