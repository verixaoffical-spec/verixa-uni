What Verixa does

Verixa reads pull requests on a repository you connect, gives each one a Low, Medium or High risk label using rules in code, and uses an AI model to write an explanation and suggest test areas. It can also store your Playwright specs and Postman collection.

Your account

  • Give accurate details, and keep your password and recovery codes safe.
  • You are responsible for what happens through your account.
  • Tell us if you think someone else has used it.

Your repositories and content

  • Connect only repositories and Jira projects you are allowed to use with an outside service.
  • You keep ownership of your code and files. You allow Verixa to process them only to provide the service, including sending parts of pull requests to an AI model provider, as described in the Privacy Policy.
  • Do not upload secrets, malicious files, or anything you have no right to share.

Acceptable use

Do not attack or overload Verixa, try to reach another person's data, get around its security or limits, or use it to break the law.

Results can be wrong

A risk label is guidance, not proof that a change is safe or unsafe. AI explanations and test suggestions can contain mistakes. Review them yourself, and keep your own testing and code review.

Availability

Verixa is provided "as is", without warranties. As a student project, it can be unavailable, change or stop at any time, and data could be lost. Keep your own copies of anything important.

Ending your use
